Frequently Asked Questions

What should I do if I find injured wildlife?

Keep your distance, keep pets away, and contact a licensed wildlife rehabilitator for instructions.

Can I keep a wild animal as a pet?

Wild animals have specialized needs and may be protected by law. Contact a qualified wildlife professional for guidance.

Can I volunteer?

Yes. Contact us to learn about current volunteer opportunities and any training that may be required.

How can I support Wild Rescues?

You can volunteer, donate, become a member, or sponsor our organization. Visit our Partnership page to learn more.

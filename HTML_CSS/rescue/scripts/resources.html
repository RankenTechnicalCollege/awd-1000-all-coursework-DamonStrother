Wildlife Resources

If you find injured or orphaned wildlife, keep a safe distance and avoid handling the animal unless a qualified wildlife professional instructs you to do so.

What You Can Do

  • Observe the animal from a safe distance.
  • Keep children and pets away.
  • Do not feed the animal or give it water unless instructed by a professional.
  • Contact a licensed wildlife rehabilitator or local animal control agency for guidance.

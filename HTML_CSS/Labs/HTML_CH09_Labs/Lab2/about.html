About Wild Rescues

Wild Rescues is a nonprofit organization dedicated to rescuing and rehabilitating injured, sick, and orphaned wildlife.

Our mission is to provide animals with the care they need and release them back into their natural habitats whenever possible.

Some animals cannot safely return to the wild. We provide ongoing care for these animals and work to give them a healthy, safe environment.

What We Do

  • Rescue injured and orphaned wildlife.
  • Provide food, shelter, and veterinary care.
  • Rehabilitate animals for release whenever possible.
  • Educate the community about protecting wildlife.

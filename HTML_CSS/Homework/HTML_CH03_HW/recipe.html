Gourmet Steak with Blueberry Purée

Ingredients

  • 2 ribeye steaks
  • 1 cup fresh blueberries
  • 2 tablespoons balsamic vinegar
  • 1 tablespoon honey
  • 2 tablespoons butter
  • 2 cloves garlic
  • 1 tablespoon olive oil
  • 1 teaspoon fresh thyme
  • Salt
  • Black pepper

Directions

  1. Take the steaks out of the refrigerator and let them sit at room temperature for 20 minutes.
  2. Season both sides of the steaks with salt and black pepper.
  3. Heat the olive oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat.
  4. Place the steaks in the skillet and cook until they reach your desired doneness.
  5. Add the butter, garlic, and thyme to the skillet.
  6. Spoon the melted butter over the steaks while they cook.
  7. Remove the steaks from the skillet and let them rest for 5 to 10 minutes.
  8. Place the blueberries, balsamic vinegar, and honey into a small saucepan.
  9. Cook the blueberry mixture until the blueberries become soft.
  10. Blend the blueberry mixture until it becomes smooth.
  11. Slice the steaks and serve them with the blueberry purée.

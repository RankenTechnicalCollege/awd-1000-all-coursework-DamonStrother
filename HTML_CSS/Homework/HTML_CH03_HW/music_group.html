Whirr

About Whirr

Whirr is a shoegaze band from Modesto, California. The band formed in 2010 and is known for its atmospheric guitar sound and dreamy style of music.

Band Members

  • Nick Bassett - Guitar
  • Loren Rivera - Vocals and Guitar
  • Joseph Bautista - Guitar
  • Eddie Salgado - Bass
  • Devin Nunes - Drums

Favorite Releases

Pipe Dreams
Pipe Dreams is one of my favorite Whirr releases because of its dreamy guitar sounds and heavy shoegaze atmosphere. The album has a hazy and emotional sound that makes it easy to listen to from beginning to end.
Sway
Sway has a heavier and more powerful sound. I like the combination of distorted guitars, vocals, and atmosphere. It is a great album for someone who enjoys loud but atmospheric music.
Feels Like You
Feels Like You has a beautiful and immersive sound. The guitars create a large wall of sound while the vocals blend into the music. This is one of my favorite Whirr albums because of its emotional atmosphere.

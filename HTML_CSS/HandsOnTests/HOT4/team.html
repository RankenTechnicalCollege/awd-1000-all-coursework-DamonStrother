CREATIVE DIRECTION

Alex Morgan

Alex guides OBSCURA's visual identity and creative direction. With an interest in architecture, proportion, and contemporary menswear, Alex works to keep each collection focused and cohesive.

The goal is to make business attire feel modern without losing the precision and polish that make tailoring timeless.

BRAND & STYLING

Jordan Lee

Jordan shapes the brand story and styling direction, bringing together versatile pieces that work across professional and formal settings. Her approach centers on thoughtful details and confident simplicity.

She believes a strong wardrobe should feel personal, practical, and refined rather than driven by short-lived trends.
